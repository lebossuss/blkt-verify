Privacy Policy — Blackout Interactive Verify

This app links your Roblox account to your account on the Blackout Interactive Discord server.

What we get: your Roblox user ID and username (scopes "openid" and "profile"). We never see your password.

What we keep: the link between your Discord ID and your Roblox ID, used to give you roles and in-game perks.

What we do not do: we do not sell or share your data, and we do not post anything on your account.

Delete your data: open a ticket on our Discord and ask staff to remove your link.
